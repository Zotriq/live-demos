# Invoices, receipts and VAT

- Owners can download every invoice as PDF from Settings → Billing → Invoices.
- To add a VAT ID or change the company name and address on future invoices, go to Settings → Billing → Billing details.
- Invoices that were already issued can be re-issued with new details once, on request from support.
- Payment methods: card on all plans; bank transfer on annual Pro and Enterprise plans.
- If a card payment fails we retry 3 times over 7 days. The workspace stays active during that time.
