# Sharing dashboards

- Share with teammates: open a dashboard → Share → add people or groups from your workspace.
- Share outside your company: Share → Public link. Public links are read-only and can be password-protected.
- Public links are turned off by workspace admins in Settings → Security → "Allow public links". If the option is greyed out, ask an admin.
- Embedding a dashboard in another site (iframe) is available on Pro and Enterprise.
